Here’s What To Do Before We Meet.

You do not need a presentation. Bring enough context for us to follow the work, find the friction and answer the questions that matter.

01

Check your booking details.

Find the confirmation and calendar invitation. Check the date, time and time zone, then keep the joining link where you can find it.

No confirmation yet? Check spam or promotions. Do not assume a slot is booked until TidyCal confirms it.

02

Bring one real workflow.

Choose a typical project and the handover that keeps creating confusion. Explain what happens today, who is involved and where the work gets stuck.

For a factory discussion, have a representative cut list in mind. You do not need to share confidential client data.

03

Bring the right context.

Be ready to explain your team size, current tools and priority. Include the person who owns that workflow when practical, so the discussion reflects daily operations.

Examples: the project manager, production lead, accounts lead or business owner responsible for the process.

04

Join ready to see the detail.

Use a laptop or desktop when possible, check your connection and join a few minutes early. Keep your questions handy while we look at relevant product workflows.

Need a different time? Use the rescheduling option in your booking confirmation rather than making a duplicate booking.
